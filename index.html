About

I'm an American filmmaker who moved to Tokyo after visiting twice and deciding I wasn't done. Everything I make — on camera or off — comes from the same instinct: slow down, pay attention, notice what's actually in front of you.

These tours are that same instinct, minus the lens. No script, no group, no checklist of ten shrines in one afternoon — just Tokyo shown to you the way I actually experience it, at a pace that lets it land.
